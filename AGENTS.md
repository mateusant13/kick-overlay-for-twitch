# AGENTS.md

## Copy and tone

- Never use the phrase **"Twitch no centro. Kick e YouTube no vídeo. Anúncios fora do caminho."** or its English translation **"Twitch at the center. Kick and YouTube in the video. Ads out of the way."** in README files, docs, UI, commit messages, or replies.
- Do not write slogan-style taglines, rhetorical questions, or AI-sounding sales copy.
- Describe features and behavior directly. No "why try it", "what you get", "sell without selling", superlatives, or promises.
- Avoid unqualified claims such as "free", "best", "no ads", or "never". State what the code does and what it does not do.
- Keep `README.md` in friendly Portuguese and `README.en.md` as the English mirror.
- Do not rename the repository. The requested product name is the README title only.
- Avoid em dashes, "slug", proxy/VPS mentions, OBS comparisons, and claims that a version is validated without evidence.
